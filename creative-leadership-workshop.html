The Journey

From curiosity to a real, shareable product experience.

Each day has a different creative job. Together, they form one continuous journey from first spark to public presentation.

Level 1: Explorer

From no idea to a high-potential innovative concept.

You will start with curiosity, select a brand, uncover its Brand DNA, and use cross-disciplinary remixing to generate innovation directions. The day moves from understanding creativity as a repeatable process to choosing a brand, identifying its Brand Skills, Brand Personality, and Brand Belief, then using those findings as the raw material for new product and service possibilities.

After exploring multiple directions, your team will evaluate, select, and synthesize one high-potential idea. You will then tell the concept from the user's first-person perspective and turn the journey into an interactive storybook website that others can see, understand, and experience.

END-OF-DAY SHAREABLE WORK Interactive Storybook Website

By the end of Day 1, you will have a visible concept experience you can show to others, not just an idea written in notes.

Level 2: Forger

From innovative idea to evidence-based MVP and a public-facing product experience.

Day 2 turns creative energy into product focus. You will clarify the user, test assumptions, discover meaningful insights, and refine your idea into an MVP with a clear problem, audience, usage context, and feature logic.

The sprint is guided by four critical questions: What problem are you solving, and for whom? How do you know they really need it? What does user behavior tell you? How should those insights reshape the product? These questions help your team move from an exciting idea to a product concept that is grounded in real user and market evidence.

AI will support research, analysis, synthesis, reasoning, and visualization, but it will not decide the product direction for you. Your team's judgment will determine which assumptions to challenge, which features belong in the MVP, and how the user journey should be rebuilt.

You will then translate the MVP White-paper into a polished product story, build a mock official website with AI-assisted coding, and integrate your user journey video.

The website should feel like the official site of a real product, not a classroom assignment. Your team will shape the product positioning, origin story, core value, feature highlights, user scenarios, visual content, and call to action into one coherent experience that people can visit and believe in.
